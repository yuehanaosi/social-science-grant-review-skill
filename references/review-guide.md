# 逐栏审稿指南 / Section Review Guide

## 选题与论证 / Topic and rationale

明确研究对象、核心问题和学科视角，按需界定地域、时段与分析单位。对照相关研究和已立项项目，写清拟解决的问题和区别。研究内容逐项对应子问题。创新落实到新增解释、证据、方法或应用。核对研究基础、进度、分工和成果，区分已完成与拟开展的工作。

Define the object, question and disciplinary perspective. Set geographic, temporal and analytical boundaries where relevant. Compare related literature and funded projects to establish the specific contribution. Map research tasks to subquestions and identify the proposed advance in explanation, evidence, method or application. Check that prior work, schedule, responsibilities and outputs agree. Distinguish completed work from planned work.

## 研究方法 / Methods

逐项说明用什么资料回答哪个问题、如何获取、怎样分析、能支持什么结论。文本研究查版本和解释路径，案例或访谈查选择依据、可接触性和分析过程，定量研究查样本、指标和识别条件。指出相关性被写成因果、样本与推论范围不符、计划超过资料获取能力等问题。

For each question, specify the evidence, access, analysis and permissible inference. For textual work, check editions and interpretation. For cases or interviews, check selection, access and analysis. For quantitative work, check the sample, measures and identification assumptions. Flag causal claims based on association, overgeneralization and plans that exceed data access.

## 文献检索与综述 / Literature search and review

按研究问题选取中外文关键词，补检时记录来源、日期、检索式和范围。区分已核全文、已核摘要、仅核题录和待核。核对作者、题名、出处和可用链接，并检查文献能否支持所引论断。仅核题录的文献不能支撑研究结论。直接引语和具体数据核原文及可用页码。综述按已知结论、争议与待解决的问题组织，核对正文引用与文末条目。

Search using Chinese and international-language terms drawn from the research question. Record source, date, query and scope when supplementing a search. Label evidence as full text, abstract, bibliographic record or unverified. Verify authorship, title, venue and available links, then match each claim to its evidence. Bibliographic records establish existence only. Check direct quotations and specific figures against the original text and pages where available. Organize the review around findings, disagreements and unanswered questions. Reconcile citations with references.

## 机器腔与矛盾 / Prose and inconsistencies

删减重复背景、空泛判断与套话。把“系统揭示运行机制”之类的表述改为能核对的研究对象、分析动作和材料。只采用申请人提供或已核实的事实。修改后复核术语、时间、数字和因果强度。正文称“拟开展访谈”、进度表称“已完成访谈”时，标出两处，说明对可行性论证的影响，待作者确认实际进度再改。

Cut repeated background, vague claims and stock phrases. Replace broad promises with a specific object, analytical action and body of evidence. Use applicant-provided or verified facts. Recheck terminology, dates, figures and causal strength after editing. If the narrative describes planned interviews but the schedule marks them complete, cite both passages, explain the effect on feasibility and revise after the applicant confirms progress.
